PrEng, BSc (Eng) Meng

Simon Griffiths

simon@lsgauteng.co.za

Simon Griffiths Graduated from the University of Cape Town in 1999 with a BSc (Eng) in Civil Engineering. Immediately after graduation he started his career with Murray and Roberts Cape as a Site Engineer. After a year with M&R in Cape Town he was transferred to the Middle East where he worked for M&R Middle East as a Site Engineer.

Upon returning to South Africa in 2001 he entered the Consulting industry with Africon Engineering International in Bloemfontein where he stayed briefly until joining Liebenberg and Stander in 2002, renamed L&S Consulting in December 2005. He graduated from the University of the Witwatersrand in 2006 with a MEng in Structural Engineering. His field of expertise lies in the structural design of concrete and steel. In 2006 he was appointed as an Associate of L&S Consulting and on the 1st of March 2009 he became a Director of L&S Consulting.

Noteworthy Projects

  • New Market Lifestyle Centre, Boardwalk Mall Gqeberha, Gezina Lifestyle Centre, Sam Ntuli Mall Katlehong, Ballito Junction, Mall of the North Polokwane, Various Warehouse Developments in Johannesburg and Cape Town
  • Atlantic Hills Warehouses Cape Town, City Lodge Newtown
  • Newtown Junction
  • Nedbank Majestic, Newtown
  • UTI Warehouse
  • Tiger Brands Head Office
  • Showrooms for Renault, AMH, Honda, Nissan, Mercedes Benz, Toyota, General Motors and Volkswagen
  • Various Retail Developments around the country
  • Numerous Builders Warehouses, The Auroras Residential development Umhlanga
  • Westlake Business Precinct Cape Town and Borouge Polyethylene plant in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates.

PrEng, BSc (Eng). GDE, MSc (Eng)

Gianfranco Talocchino

Gianfranco Talocchino graduated from the University of the Witwatersrand in 2003 with a BSc (Eng) in Civil Engineering. He continued his studies & obtained a GDE & MSc (Eng) from the University of the Witwatersrand in 2005. He joined Bateman Engineered Technologies (BET) & was involved in the design of industrial structures. He started working for L&S Consulting in 2006. His experience is in concrete & structural steel design. In 2009 he was appointed as an Associate of L&S Consulting and in 2015 became a director.

03
PrEng, B(Eng), GDE (Wits)

Christopher Bloem

christopher@lsgauteng.co.za

Christopher Bloem graduated from the University of Pretoria in 2011 with a BEng in Civil Engineering. After graduating he started his career with Steffanutti Stocks Civils, with whom he had a study bursary. He worked on the Life of Mine maintenance facility project on Sishen Mine in Kathu for a period of 2 years, where he was involved as a site engineer.

He started working for L&S Consulting 2014. He completed a GDE in structural engineering at the University of Witwatersrand in 2016. His main field of expertise are in the structural design of concrete. He was appointed as an Associate in 2017 and has subsequently been appointed as a Director of L&S Consulting.

Noteworthy Projects

  • Shopping centre developments include Eyethu Shopping Centre in Soweto, Daveyton Mall upgrades and additions in Daveyton, Chilli on Top Shopping Centre in Sunninghill, Maluti Crescent Mall in Phuthaditjhaba, Ferndale Mall in Randburg, Meadowdale Mall in Germiston. Office developments include Alice Lane Precinct (Phase 3) in Sandton, 92 Rivonia Road in Sandton, Brightwater Commons Iron Brew Studio additions and alterations to existing cinemas in Ferndale, Nicolway Offices in Bryanston. Warehouse developments include Boxer DC Warehouse in Polokwane, My Gas new office and warehouse facility in Lanseria.
04
PrEng, BEng

Jandré de Beer

Jandré de Beer Graduated from the University of Pretoria in 2011 with a BEng in Civil Engineering. He started his career in 2012 with CIVILCONSULT where he was involved in the design and site supervision of numerous civil infrastructure projects and residential township developments. He started working for L&S Consulting in 2014. His main fields of expertise are in civil infrastructure and township services related to medium and large sized residential, business and commercial developments. In 2017 he registered with ECSA as a Professional Engineer and was appointed as an Associate of L&S Consulting. In 2023 he was appointed as a Director of L&S Consutling.

Noteworthy Projects

  • Construction of roads and stormwater for Lesedi Local Municipality situated in the Bergsig X9, Heidelberg X23 & X26 and Ratanda X7 areas, Ormonde X51 in Ormonde. The relocation of two main City of Tshwane water pipelines (710 & 1000mm Ø HDPE). Partial installation of 44 750m of 2 100mm steel pipes (named the B17 pipeline) and 3 000m of 1 300mm steel pipes (named the B18 pipeline). Services infrastructure, bulk earthworks, roads and paving for the following projects: Ballito Junction Regional Mall in Ballito, Thavhani Mall in Thohoyandou, Monte Circle in Fourways, Town Square Mall in Richards Bay, Eyethu Shopping Centre in Soweto, Boardwalk Mall in Gqeberha, 92 Rivonia Road in Sandton, Allandale Offices in Midrand, My Gas in Lanseria Business Park, Italtile in Boksburg, Leroy Merlin in Boksburg, Westlake Warehouse in Linbro Park, Cornubia Ridge Logistic Park in Cornubia, Waterfall Offices in Midrand, Monte Circle in Fourways, N4 & Mandela Drive Road upgrades for Highveld Mall and Willow Grove in Little Falls.
