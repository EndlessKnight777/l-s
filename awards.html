Awards

L&S Consulting (Pty) Ltd have been awarded numerous Diamond, Gold and Silver Arrows by Professional Management Review (PMR) for Structural and Civil Engineering as well as for Engineering Design between 2000 and 2023. These awards are based on opinion surveys of Property Developers, Architects, Quantity Surveyors and Contractors. Further awards include the 2005 SAPDA award, as a winner in the industrial developments category for the Fresenius Kabi head office, in Midrand, SAPOA awards for Morningside Shopping Center (2010) and Alice Lane and UTI in 2014.
