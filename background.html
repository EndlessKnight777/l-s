L&S Consulting (Pty) Ltd 's history dates back to its origins in Cape Town, where Liebenberg & Stander Consulting Engineers was founded as a partnership in 1954 in Cape Town. A Johannesburg office was opened in 1981, and this evolved into an independent company, Liebenberg & Stander Gauteng (Pty) Ltd in 1995. In 2005, the company's name was changed to L&S Consulting (Pty) Ltd.

Our company is involved in Consulting Structural and Civil Engineering, mainly for the private sector for office buildings, retail and industrial developments as well as motor dealerships, residential and hotel projects.

Our primary business is the design of concrete, structural steel, timber and masonry building structures, as well as the design of associated roads, parking and infrastructural services. The projects we are involved in are designed to be competitively cost effective, yet reflect the quality expected by the owners. Each director is closely involved in the day to day project management, design and construction supervision of all of our projects.

L&S Consulting (Pty) Ltd is owned by the directors and associates of the company: Simon Griffiths, Gianfranco Talocchino, Christopher Bloem and Jandré de Beer.
