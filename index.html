About us

We primarily focus on the private sector, with tailored designs for office buildings, retail and industrial developments as well as motor dealerships, residential and hotel projects.

Our primary business is the design of concrete, structural steel, timber and masonry building structures, as well as the design of associated roads, parking and infrastructural services.

Services

Structural & civil engineering design.

L&S Consulting (Pty) Ltd provides a complete range of Structural and Civil Engineering design services, including:

  1. Design of structures for shopping centres, offices, hotels, warehouses, motor dealerships and similar buildings
  2. Design of bulk excavations, roads, paving, stormwater, sewers, water reticulation and other civil engineering works, in particular those related to building work
  3. CAD design and modeling using AutoCAD, Revit Structure and TechnoCAD Design Suite software
  4. Computer analysis and modeling using the most up to date structural design software. This includes Prokon and ETABS (specialist lateral stability design for high rise buildings).
  5. On-site construction monitoring and quality control
  6. Preparation of structural reports for insurance purposes
